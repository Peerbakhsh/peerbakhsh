Kabar Qism

Rate hamesha 1 Kg ka dein. 500 Gram, 250 Gram waghera ka rate system khud calculate karega.

Kabar Dene Wala (Naam) Add Karein

Jis banday se aap kabar (loha, gata, plastic waghera) khareedte hain, uska naam yahan add karein. Phir har purchase us naam se save hogi. Naam add karna zaroori nahi — chahain to seedha "Counter" se bhi khareed sakte hain.

📦 Kabar Complete Purchase Bill — Maal Stock Mein Jama

Ek hi bande se Plastic, Gatta, Loha ya jitni bhi Kabar aaye, sab ko isi ek purchase bill mein line-by-line add karein. Bill save hote hi har item ka stock jama hoga.
Pehle item add karein → phir doosra item add karein → sab items current bill mein jama hote jayenge. Aakhir mein POORA PURCHASE BILL SAVE dabayein. Stock mein asal wazan jama hoga, lekin paison ka hisab asal wazan − Wazan Riayat par hoga. Yani Riayat paison ke bill se cut hogi, stock se nahi.
Bill Paisa Hisab: Neeche isi current bill ke andar Total ke baad Bill Riayat, Jama aur Extra Rakam bharein. Har value isi bill ke total ke saath adjust hokar save hogi. Misal: Total 2000 − Riayat 100 = Net Bill 1900; Jama 1500 ho to Baqaya 400 rahega. Agli baar isi supplier ka naya bill alag hisab se chalega.
Rate wala system bhi active hai: agar Purchase Rate / Special Rate ya Total Amount se naya rate purane Buy Rate se different hua to system Rate Farq History mein record karega aur farq + munafa par asar bhi batayega. Total Amount dene par per-Kg rate khud niklega.

⚡ Kabar Cash Purchase — Baghair Wazan

Bachay ya koi banda kachra de aur aap sirf Rs. 10, 20, 50 waghera dein to yahan entry karein. Di hui rakam Actual Purchase Cost mein zaroor jayegi, lekin abhi KG stock mein nahi jayega. Plastic ka paisa Plastic ke pending cost mein, Loha ka paisa Loha mein, Gatta ka paisa Gatta mein rahega.

⚖️ Baghair Wazan Kabaad Ko Baad Mein KG Stock Mein Daalna

Jab bachon se liya hua jama maal baad mein tol lein, yahan usi Kabar Qism ka total wazan likhein. Us qism ki pending Cash Cost bhi stock ki cost mein add hogi. Misal: Loha ke Rs. 500 pending thay aur baad mein 20 KG mila → Stock +20 KG aur Cost +Rs. 500. Is se pehle se maujood 100 KG ke saath average actual cost automatically mix ho jayegi.

🧾 Kabar Complete Bill

Ek bill mein Loha 1 Kg 500 Gram, Plastic 2 Kg 250 Gram, Gata waghera sab aik saath add karein. Rate sirf 1 Kg ka dena hoga. System Gram ko automatically Kg mein convert karega. Stock kam ya zyada hone ki wajah se Kabar bill kabhi block nahi hoga.

⚡ Fast Add — Kai Qism Ek Sath Bill Mein

Tareeqa: Jin qismon ki quantity chahiye, un sab cards mein Kg/Gram likhein (checkbox khud tick ho jayega), phir neeche wala button dabayein — sab ek hi baar mein Kabar Bill mein add ho jayengi. Ek qism ke liye us card ka apna ➕ Bill Mein Add bhi use kar sakte hain.

✍️ Manual — Ek Qism Add Karein

Misal: 1 Kg + 500 Gram = 1.5 Kg. Agar rate Rs 200/Kg hai to total Rs 300. Special Total Amount diya ho to woh priority par use hoga.

💰 Bill Ka Final Hisaab — Riayat / Extra / Jama

Pehle items add karein. Jab Poora Bill ban jaye, isi jagah Riayat likhein. Riayat likhte hi Net Bill aur Baqaya automatic kam ho jayega. Agar aap ne customer ko Extra Rakam Di hai to woh bhi yahin likhein. Jama bhi bill ke waqt isi jagah likh sakte hain.
